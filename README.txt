Kyoka Suigetsu addon for Bleach Awaken (Forge 1.16.5)

YOUR FILES GO HERE:
- Sealed sword texture (16x16 PNG):  src/main/resources/assets/kyoka/textures/item/kyoka_suigetsu_sealed.png
- Released sword texture (16x16 PNG): src/main/resources/assets/kyoka/textures/item/kyoka_suigetsu.png

Item models are already in src/main/resources/assets/kyoka/models/item/.
Names are in src/main/resources/assets/kyoka/lang/.

CONTROLS: hold the item in your MAIN HAND and press J to switch sealed <-> released.

BUILD (needs a PC with JDK 8 and Gradle, or GitHub Actions):
  ./gradlew build
The jar appears in build/libs/. Put it in the mods folder together with bleachawaken.
